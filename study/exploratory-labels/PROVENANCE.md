# Provenance of the Reviewer A and Reviewer B labels

On 2026-10-22 the repository owner, Aymen Jedidi, stated that the Reviewer A
and Reviewer B label passes in this folder were made by hand by two humans,
himself and Ahmed Amine Essid, and are not AI work:

- `ai-shadow-review/` and `teammate-review/`: the two passes over the
  350-item agreement pilot;
- `annotation-comparison/`: the comparisons built on them.

Some files and commit messages describe these passes as AI-generated (for
example "labeled by: AI (Claude)" or "AI precheck"). That wording is left
unchanged as part of the record. This note records the owner's statement
next to it.

These passes are separate from the formal human validation in
`study/validation-v007-human/`.
