# Facilitator checklist

1. Assign two humans to independent source and output review. Keep original
   returned forms read-only and dated; record reviewers' identities privately.
2. Distribute each reviewer only source-review/ and their own CSV, reviewer
   instructions and protocol. The package manifest contains engineering metadata:
   keep it and all model outputs out of initial review packets.
3. After independent source review, resolve disagreements and complete the
   separate source-adjudication form. Do not modify source packets or frozen
   code to improve evaluation results. A changed candidate needs a new study.
4. Export blinded output packets with the tooling. Keep the mapping key private.
   Distribute each reviewer's form separately. Archive returned forms before
   adjudication. Do not substitute AI judgments for either human.
5. For repairs, finish intended-behavior review before inference. Run scoped
   inference, mechanical checks and container probes; then obtain independent
   semantic judgments. Record not-proposed/indeterminate cases honestly.
6. For comprehension, finalize the three per-task scoring rubric fields before
   inference. The participant condition adding an LLM must show the same source
   evidence and backend facts as evidence-only. Retain failed explanations.
7. Before recruitment, finalize the consent sheet, institutional review
   determination, contact, retention and deletion arrangements, compensation if
   any, and fixed closing date. Record recruitment.json status=open only then.
8. Recruit adult developers with Ansible experience, excluding gold reviewers.
   Allocate IDs sequentially P01–P18. Keep any identity-to-ID list separately.
   The six schedules are balanced in groups of six. Log withdrawals, partial
   sessions and deviations; never recruit selectively based on outcomes.
9. Session: consent and eligibility, years of Ansible experience, unrelated
   practice task, 12 timed tasks, then debrief. Use the supplied offline page.
   No external assistants; source reading within the page is available to all.
   Ask the participant not to reload; they can stop and export a partial session.
10. Collect downloaded JSON privately. Preserve raw submissions unchanged. Import
    all submissions together, with --allow-partial when needed; partials are kept
    outside the complete-session analysis. Do not fabricate missing responses.
11. Distribute grading packets, assigned grader CSV and frozen task rubrics;
    withhold response/assignment keys, times and confidence. Preserve independent
    grades and adjudicate separately before analysis.
12. Analyze all completed assigned tasks, including wrong answers and timeouts.
    Report incomplete sessions and recruitment deviations. The 12–18 target is
    exploratory; results may show no benefit or harm. Do not promote faster wrong
    answers into a claim of improved comprehension.

Practice example: show a non-study debug task printing a harmless literal, with
an unrelated style warning. Explain the three response fields using that example,
without revealing answers to study tasks. Answer procedure questions consistently
and record substantive assistance as a deviation.
